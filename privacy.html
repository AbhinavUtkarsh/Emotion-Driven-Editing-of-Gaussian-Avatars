What is collected

These pages set no cookies, run no analytics and contain no tracking. Loading them does cause your browser to contact the providers listed under Recipients below, as happens with any website.

Submitting the access request form sends the name, email address, affiliation and message you typed, together with which document you asked for. Nothing else is read from your browser. The form contains one hidden decoy field used to catch automated spam; a person leaves it empty and it is discarded.

Entering a password to unlock a document checks it against a stored one-way hash. The password is not logged and not retained.

Purpose and legal basis

The data is used only to read your request, decide on it, and reply to you. It is not sold, not shared, and not used to contact you about anything else.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, steps taken at your request, and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in controlling access to unpublished academic work.

Storage and retention

A copy of each request is held for 90 days and then deleted automatically. It exists so that a request is not lost if the notification email fails to arrive.

The notification email is delivered to my personal mailbox and remains there, under my control, until I delete it. There is no automatic deletion of received mail. On request I will delete it.

Recipients and processors

GitHub serves these pages and records requests for them as any web server does.

Cloudflare runs the access service, holds the 90-day copy, and stores the documents. As the operator carrying the traffic it necessarily sees your IP address. What is written to storage is only a counter keyed to a cryptographic hash of that address, kept for at most 15 minutes to limit password guessing; the address itself is never stored.

Resend delivers the notification email. Mail for this domain is sent from their Ireland region, though Resend is a United States company and access from outside the EU cannot be ruled out.

Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when loading these pages. This is the only third party contacted.

Cookies

No cookies are set and no analytics are used. Your dark or light mode choice is kept in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device. Clearing site data removes it.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction of use, a portable copy, and you may object to the use of your data. As the only data held is what you typed into the form, deletion is straightforward and will be carried out promptly.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
